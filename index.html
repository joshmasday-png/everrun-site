See it work

One buyer, seven minutes

Screenshots from a test chat with our prototype, from the first message to a booked viewing and an offer on the table. Alongside it: what an experienced agent would notice.

  1. Answers, then asks for the viewing.

    Everything a buyer wants in the first reply: tenure, layout, price, MRT, schools and why this unit is good. Then two real times, taken from the agent's free calendar slots.

  2. Honest, then qualifies.

    It doesn't oversell a 2-bed to a family of four. Then it asks the two questions that decide an upgrader's timeline: is the MOP done, and are they selling first?

  3. Does the maths both ways.

    A PR upgrader pays very different ABSD depending on whether the HDB is sold first: 5% or 30%. It gives both figures, marks them as estimates and keeps qualifying.

  4. Holds the price.

    It never guesses or reveals the owner's bottom line. It asks for the buyer's number instead.

  5. Takes the offer, passes it on.

    The offer is logged and flagged to the agent straight away. Negotiation stays with a person.

  6. Books it.

    The viewing goes into the agent's calendar and the buyer gets a one-line confirmation, with the offer in it.

Failsafes

What if something goes wrong?

It's the first thing agents ask us. Your name is on every message, so these safety nets are built in from day one.

  1. It tries again first. If the AI service has a hiccup, the reply is retried automatically before anything else happens.
  2. A safe reply, never silence. If a reply still can't be used, the buyer gets a short "let me check and get back to you shortly", and you get an alert to step in.
  3. Every reply is checked twice. A second AI reviews each reply against your listings before it's sent. Anything wrong is corrected, never sent as it was.
  4. No double bookings. If a buyer asks for a time you're not free, it doesn't book it. The viewing is passed to you to arrange.
  1. You can take over at any time. Step into any chat whenever you want, and the AI stops replying to that buyer until you hand it back.
  2. Sensitive topics come to you. Offers, co-broke terms, complaints and anything legal are passed straight to you.
  3. Everything is on record. Every message in and out is saved to your own sheet, so you can see exactly what was said and when.
  4. Your setup is separate. Every agent runs on their own copy, with their own listings and calendar. A problem elsewhere never touches yours.
